# Data and sources

Everything this site knows about a eulogy, apart from its text, comes from an open registry that anyone
can use. The texts of the 2004 editions are under copyright and are not in it.

## The registry (CRMEDR)

The Common Roman Martyrology Eulogy Data Repository,
[CRMEDR](https://github.com/CatholicOS/crmedr), is a public repository of open data. It holds:

- the **identifiers** of every eulogy, current and deprecated, with the day, the entry number, the
  asterisk and the edition each is attested in (see [Canonical eulogy IDs](/docs/ids));
- the **subject** of every eulogy, the saint or celebration it is about, in Latin, Italian and English;
- the **typology** of each date: whether it marks a *dies natalis*, a burial, a translation of relics,
  a finding, a dedication, and so on;
- the **places** each eulogy names, with their role (place of death, see, burial…), and a **gazetteer**
  that resolves each place to a Wikidata item and its modern country, which drives the
  [map](/map);
- the **misprints** the curators have verified in the printed editions, and the **curator notes** (see
  [Notes, misprints and errata](/docs/notes-and-marks)).

## What is not in it

The copyrighted texts of the 2004 editions are deliberately absent: the registry holds only structural
facts about the eulogies. The one exception is the place names, which are facts and are quoted as
printed. Citation-length openings of eulogies appear only in the registry's working report, to identify
entries.

## The API

The site reads its texts and data through a public API. Its reference, with every endpoint and a way to
try them, is at [/scalar](/scalar).
