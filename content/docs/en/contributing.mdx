# How to contribute

This site is the work of a few people, on a book of thousands of eulogies in several editions. It needs
more eyes. If you are a scholar or a student of liturgy, hagiography, Latin or church history, your
review can make it better for everyone who reads it.

## What needs review

- **The texts.** Do the eulogy texts on this site match the printed originals, word for word?
- **Misprints.** Have you found a misprint in an edition that the site does not yet mark with
  *[sic! expected: …]*? (See [Notes, misprints and errata](/docs/notes-and-marks).)
- **The identifiers.** Does an identifier follow the [rules](/docs/ids)? And above all, can you
  recognize the eulogy from it: is it clear who is meant, and which one?
- **Run-in eulogies.** Are there places where two eulogies have been run together as one, and need to be
  split?
- **Subjects.** Is the subject of a eulogy, the saint or celebration it names, given correctly in Latin,
  Italian and English?
- **Places.** Is each place a eulogy names identified correctly, on the [map](/map) and in its data?
- **Anything else** that is wrong, unclear or missing.

## How

Tools for curation are **coming soon** to this site: enabled users will be able to propose corrections
directly from the page they are reading. This page will link them as soon as they are ready.
